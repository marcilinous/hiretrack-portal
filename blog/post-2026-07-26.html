Bengaluru Data/MIS Salaries

Published 26 July 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Entry-level Data Analysts in Bengaluru can expect salaries between ₹4-6 lakhs per annum.
  • Mid-level Data Analysts can earn between ₹8-12 lakhs per annum.
  • Demand for skilled Data/MIS professionals is high in Bengaluru's SME market.

Bengaluru, being the IT hub of India, offers a wide range of job opportunities for Data/MIS professionals. The salaries for these roles vary based on factors like experience, skills, and industry. Typically, entry-level Data Analysts in Bengaluru can expect salaries between ₹4-6 lakhs per annum, while mid-level professionals can earn between ₹8-12 lakhs per annum.

Salary Ranges for Data/MIS Roles in Bengaluru

Role Salary Range (in lakhs) Demand
Entry-level Data Analyst 4-6 High
MIS Executive 5-7 Moderate
Mid-level Data Analyst 8-12 Very High
Senior Data Analyst 15-20 High

Factors Affecting Salaries

  • Industry: Salaries can vary based on the industry, with finance and healthcare sectors tend to offer higher salaries.
  • Skills: Proficiency in tools like Excel, SQL, and data visualization can increase salary potential.
  • Experience: More experienced professionals can expect higher salaries, with some mid-level roles offering up to ₹15 lakhs per annum.
