Bengaluru's Hiring Hotspots: Whitefield, Electronic City, Koramangala

Published 4 August 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Whitefield, Electronic City, and Koramangala are top hiring hotspots in Bengaluru
  • Typical salary ranges for Data Analyst roles: ₹5-15 lakhs per annum
  • SMEs in these areas often require skilled Data Analysts and MIS professionals

Bengaluru, being the IT hub of India, has numerous hiring hotspots that attract both job seekers and SMEs. Among these, Whitefield, Electronic City, and Koramangala stand out as the most sought-after destinations. These areas are home to a plethora of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) that are constantly on the lookout for skilled professionals, particularly in the Data Analyst and MIS domains.

Hiring Trends in Whitefield, Electronic City, and Koramangala

These three areas have experienced significant growth in recent years, with many SMEs setting up their operations here. As a result, the demand for skilled professionals has increased, with Data Analyst and MIS roles being the most in-demand. According to estimates, the typical salary range for Data Analyst roles in these areas is between ₹5-15 lakhs per annum, depending on experience and skills.
