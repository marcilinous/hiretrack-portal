Data/MIS Salaries Bengaluru

Published 9 August 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Entry-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru typically offer ₹5-8 LPA
  • Mid-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru can earn ₹12-20 LPA
  • Demand for Data Analysts and MIS professionals is high across Bengaluru's SME sector

As of 2026, the typical salary range for entry-level Data/MIS professionals in Bengaluru is around ₹5-8 LPA, while mid-level roles can command salaries between ₹12-20 LPA. These estimates are based on current market trends and may vary depending on factors like company size, industry, and specific job requirements. For instance, a Data Analyst at a small to medium-sized enterprise (SME) in Bengaluru might start at around ₹6 LPA, while a similar role at a larger firm could offer upwards of ₹10 LPA.

Key Factors Influencing Salaries

  • Company size and type: Larger firms and those in the finance or technology sectors tend to offer higher salaries.
  • Industry: Certain industries like healthcare or e-commerce may offer higher salaries due to the high demand for data-driven insights.
  • Skills and qualifications: Professionals with specialized skills like data science, machine learning, or cloud computing can command higher salaries.
