Bengaluru SME Hiring Trends Q3 2026

Published 29 July 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Bengaluru's SME sector is witnessing a surge in demand for Data Analysts
  • Typical salary ranges for Data Analysts in Bengaluru's SMEs are between ₹5-10 lakhs per annum
  • MIS roles are in high demand, with around 200-300 job openings in Q3 2026

Bengaluru, being the IT hub of India, is experiencing a significant spike in hiring trends in the SME sector, particularly for Data Analyst and MIS roles. As of Q3 2026, the demand for skilled professionals in these areas is on the rise. According to industry estimates, around 500-700 job openings are available for Data Analysts and MIS professionals in Bengaluru's SME sector.

Current Hiring Trends

The current hiring trends in Bengaluru's SME sector indicate a strong demand for professionals with expertise in data analysis, reporting, and business intelligence. The typical salary ranges for these roles are between ₹5-15 lakhs per annum, depending on the experience and skills of the candidate.

Challenges and Opportunities

While the demand for Data Analysts and MIS professionals is high, there are also challenges that companies face in hiring the right talent. The competition for skilled professionals is intense, and companies need to offer competitive salaries and benefits to attract the best candidates. However, this also presents an opportunity for job seekers to negotiate better salaries and benefits, and for companies to invest in training and development programs to upskill their existing employees.
