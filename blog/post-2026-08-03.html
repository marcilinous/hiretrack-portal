Key Takeaways

Bengaluru, being the IT hub of India, has a high demand for Data Analysts and MIS professionals with expertise in Excel, SQL, and Power BI. Employers in the SME sector are looking for candidates who can efficiently manage and analyze data to drive business decisions. In Bengaluru, employers want Data Analysts with strong Excel skills, including data manipulation, pivot tables, and chart creation. SQL knowledge is also essential for managing and querying databases. Additionally, Power BI skills are in high demand for creating interactive and dynamic visualizations.

While prior experience with SQL is not always necessary, it is highly desirable and can be a key factor in the hiring decision.

You can learn Power BI on your own through online tutorials and practice, but formal training can provide a more comprehensive understanding of the tool and its applications.

How important are Excel skills for a Data Analyst role?

Excel skills are crucial for a Data Analyst role, as they are used for data manipulation, analysis, and reporting.
