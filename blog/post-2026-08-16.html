Bengaluru Data/MIS Salaries

Published 16 August 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Entry-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru pay around ₹4-7 LPA
  • Mid-level Data/MIS salaries range from ₹8-15 LPA
  • Demand for Data/MIS professionals is high in Bengaluru's SME sector

Typical entry-level Data/MIS salaries in Bengaluru range from ₹4-7 LPA, while mid-level roles offer ₹8-15 LPA. These estimates are based on current market trends and can vary depending on factors like company size, industry, and specific job requirements. Bengaluru's SME sector is a significant driver of this demand, with many companies relying on data-driven insights to inform business decisions.

Key Factors Influencing Salaries

  • Company size and type: Larger companies tend to offer higher salaries, while startups may offer more variable compensation packages.
  • Industry: Certain industries, such as finance and healthcare, may offer higher salaries for Data/MIS roles due to the sensitive nature of the data being handled.
  • Specific job requirements: Roles requiring specialized skills, such as data science or machine learning, may command higher salaries.
