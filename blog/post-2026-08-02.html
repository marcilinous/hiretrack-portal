Data/MIS Salaries Bengaluru: Entry vs Mid-Level

Published 2 August 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Entry-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru typically offer ₹5-8 LPA
  • Mid-level Data/MIS roles offer ₹10-18 LPA, with some roles reaching up to ₹22 LPA
  • Demand for Data/MIS professionals is high in Bengaluru's SME sector

When it comes to Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru, salary ranges can vary significantly depending on the level of experience. Typical entry-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru offer ₹5-8 LPA, while mid-level roles offer ₹10-18 LPA. For hiring managers and job seekers looking to navigate the Bengaluru job market, understanding these salary ranges is crucial.

In Bengaluru, the salary ranges for Data/MIS roles can be broken down into several categories. The following table provides an overview of typical salary ranges for entry-level and mid-level Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru.

Factors Affecting Salary Ranges

Several factors can affect the salary ranges for Data/MIS roles in Bengaluru, including the company size, industry, and level of experience. Additionally, the demand for Data/MIS professionals in Bengaluru's SME sector is high, which can drive up salary ranges. Some of the key factors affecting salary ranges include:

  • Company size: Larger companies tend to offer higher salaries than smaller companies
  • Industry: Certain industries, such as finance and healthcare, tend to offer higher salaries than others
  • Level of experience: More experienced professionals can command higher salaries
  • Demand: High demand for Data/MIS professionals can drive up salary ranges
