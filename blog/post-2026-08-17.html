Published 17 August 2026 · HireTrack

Key Takeaways

  • Excel skills are a must for data analysis in Bengaluru SMEs

  • Power BI expertise is in demand for data visualization

In Bengaluru's thriving SME market, Data Analysts and MIS professionals with expertise in Excel, SQL, and Power BI are in high demand. Employers typically look for candidates who can collect, analyze, and visualize data to inform business decisions. With the rise of data-driven decision making, these skills have become essential for businesses to stay competitive.

Key Skills for Data Analysts in Bengaluru

Bengaluru's SMEs require Data Analysts to have a strong foundation in Excel, including data manipulation, pivot tables, and chart creation. SQL skills are also highly valued, as they enable data management and analysis. Additionally, Power BI expertise is sought after for creating interactive and dynamic visualizations.
